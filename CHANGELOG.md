# Starforge Changelog

Starforge is an open-world space exploration game inspired by *Starflight* (1986).
Version numbers started appearing in the game at 0.72. Changes before that are grouped by milestone.
A version ending in .1, .2 and so on (like 0.76.1) changes only the manual or About screen, not the game itself.

---

## 0.81
- **Rover upgrades rebalanced.** Each level is a smaller step, so a fully upgraded rover is strong but no longer unstoppable.
- Your rover's upgrades now show on the Ship tab.
- A new tall tale in the lounges.

## 0.80
- **Stranded on foot.** If your rover runs out of power or is wrecked far from the lander, your captain has to abandon it and walk back in a space suit before the air runs out.
- A new tall tale in the lounges.

## 0.79.1
- New tall tales in lounges, cantinas and taverns.
- Planets no longer list ruins once you've explored them all.
- Fixed the Codex count shown when loading a save.

## 0.79
- **Name your captain** when you start a new game.
- **Hall of Captains** at your home starbase, honoring captains lost in service.
- **Exploring on foot.** Some places the rover can't reach. Step out in a space suit and walk. Better suits are sold at the shipyard.
- **New chapters in the main story.**
- Ruins no longer appear on gas giants.
- **Fixed:** pressing Enter on a planet's surface could land you again and speed up the rover.
- Balance improvements.

## 0.78
- **Stardate.** Today's date now shows as a Stardate just above the map coordinates, and everywhere the game used to show the day.
- **Ship skins.** Ten hull designs and six engine glows in the shipyard. They change only how your ship looks, at any upgrade level.
- **A hidden tribute** to a beloved captain, waiting to be found.
- **Every species has its own look.** The Krillians, Skarr, Thexx, Selenn, Drovath, Quarn and Myrr have all been redrawn.
- You can no longer colonize a planet that already has an alien colony.
- Friendly visitors in the starport corridor now look like their own species.

## 0.77.1
- Updated title screen text.

## 0.77
- **More realistic people.** Everyone in the game has been redrawn, from your crew to the peoples beyond the frontier.
- **New ways to spend credits:**
  - Crew quarters and a med bay for your ship.
  - Paint schemes, and your ship's name painted on the hull.
  - Starbase improvements: a bigger shipyard, a refinery, or a bigger lounge.
  - Funding your colonies so they grow faster.
  - Star charts, tips and other information from bartenders and colony officials.
  - Insurance, 25 credits a week.
- **One set of action keys.** Z, X and C (or , . /) now do the main jobs everywhere: on the tactical screen, on planets, and in the spaceport.
- Balance improvements.

## 0.76
- **Title screen.** Choose Captain or Builder when the game opens. A Title link under the Starforge name brings you back to it.
- **Colonies you can visit.** Alien species have colonies in their own space, each with a trade post, a colony hall and a cantina. Friendly and Neutral species let you land for free, Wary ones charge a landing fee, and Hostile ones turn you away.
- **Your own colonies** can be visited too: buy fuel, sell metal, check on their progress, and have a drink with the colonists.
- **Conversations.** After hailing a ship you can ask about who they are, their homeworld, trade, news, their neighbors and local dangers.
- **Wanted goods.** Each species pays extra for certain items. Find out what they want and it's noted in your Known species list.
- **Surrender.** Beaten ships may give up. Board them, let them go, or finish them. A captured ship can be towed home and sold at your starbase.
- **Cantina talk.** Travelers share stories from beyond the frontier, along with plenty of gossip that's no use to anyone.
- New faces in the cantinas. Some of them offer work.
- Manual updated with the new features.

## 0.75
- The game now checks for updates on its own GitHub page.
- Downloading an update carries your saved game into the new file.

## 0.73 – 0.74
- **About** page: the game's inspiration, how it was made, and a short synopsis.
- **Captain's Manual**, in the game and as a separate document: the known species, background lore, ship and crew, the spaceport, exploring, encounters, controls and saving.
- **Automatic backup** of your save, so clearing your browser's data doesn't erase your game. The game can keep a backup file up to date for you.
- **Online updater** (first version).
- **Fixed:** planet maps where the rover couldn't reach some ruins or deposits.

## Early development (before 0.72)

### The galaxy
- Galaxy builder for generating and editing star systems, with mostly random and custom generation.
- A galaxy that grows as you reach its edge.
- Star and planet names no longer overlap on the map.
- Better space visuals.

### Ship and crew
- Your own ship, with fuel, hull, shields, weapons and upgrades.
- Shield and weapon buttons, plus hotkeys: 1 for shields, 2 for weapons.
- A crew with seven skills, station assignments and hit points.
- The doctor heals injured crew. Crew can die, and if the captain falls, command passes down the chain.
- Crew sometimes pick up skill points chatting in the lounge.

### Exploring
- System and planet scans.
- Planet landings with a rover: mining, exploring ruins and capturing wildlife.
- Every animal species looks unique.
- Recommend worlds for colonies, with a warning before you recommend a poor one.
- Build fuel depots, outposts and starbases (but not on a planet that already has one).

### Aliens and encounters
- Alien species with their own territories, attitudes and rivalries. Species can fight each other.
- Friendly starbases trade with you, and neutral ones might.
- Tactical encounter screen: maneuver, pick a target among several ships, and fire lasers (Z or ,) and torpedoes (X or .).
- Scan alien ships to see their weapons, shields, cargo and fuel. They notice the scan, and it changes how they treat you.
- Arming weapons or raising shields also changes how other ships react.
- Shields wear down when hit and recharge after each encounter.
- Scavenge wreckage after a fight.
- Distress calls from ships under attack stay open until you next dock.

### The spaceport
- A walkable spaceport with a shipyard, trade depot, personnel office, lounge and observation deck.
- Lore and rumors to collect from ruins, spacers and alien envoys.
